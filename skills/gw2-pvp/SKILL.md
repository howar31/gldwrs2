# gw2-pvp

Commands under `gw2 pvp`.

## Commands

- `gw2 pvp amulets [flags]` — Fetch pvp amulets
  - `--all` — fetch every entry (explicit; may be large)
  - `--ids <string>` — comma-separated ids (omit to list ids)
- `gw2 pvp games [flags]` — Fetch pvp games
  - `--all` — fetch every entry (explicit; may be large)
  - `--ids <string>` — comma-separated ids (omit to list ids)
- `gw2 pvp heroes [flags]` — Fetch pvp heroes
  - `--all` — fetch every entry (explicit; may be large)
  - `--ids <string>` — comma-separated ids (omit to list ids)
- `gw2 pvp ranks [flags]` — Fetch pvp ranks
  - `--all` — fetch every entry (explicit; may be large)
  - `--ids <string>` — comma-separated ids (omit to list ids)
- `gw2 pvp rewardtracks [flags]` — Fetch pvp rewardtracks
  - `--all` — fetch every entry (explicit; may be large)
  - `--ids <string>` — comma-separated ids (omit to list ids)
- `gw2 pvp runes [flags]` — Fetch pvp runes
  - `--all` — fetch every entry (explicit; may be large)
  - `--ids <string>` — comma-separated ids (omit to list ids)
- `gw2 pvp seasons [flags]` — Fetch pvp seasons
  - `--all` — fetch every entry (explicit; may be large)
  - `--ids <string>` — comma-separated ids (omit to list ids)
- `gw2 pvp seasons leaderboards` — Fetch pvp seasons leaderboards
- `gw2 pvp sigils [flags]` — Fetch pvp sigils
  - `--all` — fetch every entry (explicit; may be large)
  - `--ids <string>` — comma-separated ids (omit to list ids)
- `gw2 pvp standings` — Your PvP season standings
- `gw2 pvp stats` — Your aggregate PvP stats

See `gw2-shared` for auth setup and global flags.
