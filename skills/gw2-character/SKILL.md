# gw2-character

Commands under `gw2 character`.

## Commands

- `gw2 character [flags]` — Your characters and their details
  - `--active` — with buildtabs or equipmenttabs, fetch only the active tab (appends /active to the path)

See `gw2-shared` for auth setup and global flags.
